About the artwork

Design & sources

These illustrations come from my RegimeWeb project. The Hinduism (Om), Sikhism (Khanda), and Daoism (Yin and Yang) symbols were downloaded from public-domain sources. They are included for context and are not presented as my original drawings.

The Shaiva, Shakti, and Vaishnava designs incorporate that same public-domain Om symbol. The other designs in this selection are my work.

Names and groupings follow the project’s asset collection. The SVG files preserve their original geometry and can be viewed individually at any scale.
